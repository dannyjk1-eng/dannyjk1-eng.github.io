Put an app on your home screen

Each app installs on its own, so open the app first, then install it from there (not from this page):

  1. Android (Chrome): open the app, tap ⋮, then Install app.
  2. iPhone (Safari): open the app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Once an app has been opened online, it works offline.
